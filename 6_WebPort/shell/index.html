Zeliard Controls

Keyboard

← ↑ ↓ →Move; navigate menus
SpaceAttack; talk; confirm; advance text; resume
AltCast selected magic; secondary action; answer No
EnterOpen or close inventory
EscPause; close pause screen
F1Toggle music
F2Toggle sound effects
F7Open the load-game menu
F9Open the game-speed menu
Ctrl + Shift + S + EShow Level and EXP in the inventory screen
At the bank↑ +1, ↓ −1, ← +10, → −10. Hold a direction to accelerate.
Letters / numbersEnter saved-game names and menu values
BackspaceErase the previous typed character

Standard gamepad

D-pad / left stickMove; navigate menus
AAttack; confirm; resume
BCast magic; secondary action; answer No
XOpen or close inventory
StartPause; close pause screen
BackOpen the load-game menu
YOpen the game-speed menu
LB / RBToggle music / sound effects

Testing controls are available below the game: Invincible, Restore Health + Shield + Magic, add any witchcraft item, and Flying. They intentionally have no keyboard shortcuts.
